What We're Doing

CDW designs and builds the VCF 9.1 foundation for Meridian Health, proves the runbook and hands the platform over, and Meridian Health's team repeats it. The plan is framed around operations and lifecycle management and resilience and recovery planning, with a separately funded greenfield VCF foundation and workload migration path.

In This Plan

  • Program Management & Governance: Hardware Compatibility Review, Kickoff and Delivery Plan
  • Modernize Infrastructure: Reference Architecture Design, Migration and Cutover Plan, Greenfield VCF Foundation Build
  • Operations: VCF Operations Design, Optimization and Advisory Services, Role and Access Mapping, Training and Knowledge Transfer, VCF Operations Licensing, Handover and Service Acceptance
  • Modernize Security & Resilience: Recovery and Resilience Design

Not in This Plan

  • Modernize Infrastructure: First Workload Domain, Workload Migration and Cutover, after the entitlement
  • Operations: Health, Logs, and Diagnostics, Runbook Knowledge Transfer, after the entitlement
  • Modernize Cloud: not requested (Kubernetes, Private AI and HCX)
  • Modernize Security & Resilience: Recovery Services Build, after the entitlement
  • Run by Meridian Health: Site prerequisites and hardware evidence; Customer migration waves and legacy cluster retirement

Architecture at a Glance

PlatformVCF 9.1 on Cisco UCS hosts. Converged management domain: management components and production workloads share the primary cluster. Primary data center and a new DR site.
Storage, networkFibre Channel. NSX deployed with VCF, VLAN-backed at first.
RecoveryDR site as a separate workload domain in the same fleet.
