VCF Design Profile

Architecture summarized across the standard design profile categories. Decisions marked for design are set in the design sessions; for this pathway they include approved capital funding, management-domain topology, principal storage, physical network, migration-wave scope.

Profile CategoryAcme Health Design Decision
ConsumptionVCF 9.1 with VCF Operations as the daily front door; vCenter consumption unchanged for the team; automation evaluated later.
Physical Site ConfigurationPrimary data center and a new DR site.
AvailabilityConverged management domain: management components and production workloads share the primary cluster; HA and fully automated DRS retained.
IsolationNSX deployed with VCF, VLAN-backed at first; no network redesign on day one.

Per-component requirements with the recommendation for Acme Health. Gates are called out.

ComponentRequirementRecommendation

The technical items to confirm early

  • The VCF Installer deploys vCenter, NSX and VCF Operations with the management domain — VCF 9.1 documentation > Overview > VCF Installer Overview
