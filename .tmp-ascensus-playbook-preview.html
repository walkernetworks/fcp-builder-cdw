Customer background

Ascensus

Ascensus is preparing a delivery-led VCF adoption that converts confirmed outcomes into an implementation sequence, operating-model transition, and service handover. The plan is framed around operations and lifecycle management, automation and self-service foundations, the explicitly selected VCF networking and NSX scope and private AI enablement, with an in-place modernization path.
