Summary

CDW designs and builds the VCF 9.1 foundation for Acme Health, runs the migration waves and hands the platform over. The plan is framed around operations and lifecycle management and resilience and recovery planning, with a separately funded greenfield VCF foundation and workload migration path.

Situation

  • 14 ESXi hosts across 1 vCenter and 1 cluster
  • DR site being built: 8 new hosts and a NetApp array fed by SnapMirror; recovery time objective and orchestration not yet defined

Plan on a Page

In This Plan

  • Program Management & Governance: Hardware Compatibility Review, Kickoff and Delivery Plan
  • Modernize Infrastructure: Reference Architecture Design, Migration and Cutover Plan, Greenfield VCF Foundation Build
  • Operations: VCF Operations Design, Role and Access Mapping, Training and Knowledge Transfer, VCF Operations Licensing, Health, Logs, and Diagnostics, Handover and Service Acceptance

Not in This Plan

  • Modernize Security & Resilience: Recovery and Resilience Design, Recovery Services Build, after the entitlement

VCF Design Profile

AreaDesign
ConsumptionVCF 9.1 with VCF Operations as the daily front door; vCenter consumption unchanged for the team; automation evaluated later.
Physical sitePrimary data center and a new DR site.
AvailabilityConverged management domain: management components and production workloads share the primary cluster; HA and fully automated DRS retained.
IsolationNSX deployed with VCF, VLAN-backed at first; no network redesign on day one.
RecoverabilityDR site as a separate workload domain in the same fleet; replication with an orchestration tool to be chosen.
ExpansionEvacuated hosts rebuilt into VCF wave by wave; the DR workload domain added once the site is online.
