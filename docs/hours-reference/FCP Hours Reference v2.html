How v2 builds hours

Pass 1 prices every line from topology and per-unit counts. Pass 2 fits that scope to the entitlement by placing it in buckets; it never changes a Pass 1 value.

lead role = base hours from the line's formula
secondary technical role = 10% of base (20% on X11, X12, X16; 0 on DX)
project manager = 15% of all lead + secondary hours, at least 24 on a build, 8 on design only
add-ons (X2 to X16, EF) x depth: Tier 1 1.0, Tier 2 1.25, Tier 3 1.6
design-only and undecided pathways keep 25% of each add-on

Entitlement fit

  • Entitlement hours = entitlement value / blended rate (the rate card unless the planner overrides it), or the signed entitled hours when recorded.
  • 8% is held in reserve before filling. The fillable budget is the rest; hours left after the fill are reported as entitlement remaining.
  • E1 (core): discovery, design, the pathway spine, dated migration waves, Operations Tier 1. E2 (depth): outcomes at Tier 1 in the customer's ranking, remaining waves, depth uplifts, efficiency. The fill stops at the first whole unit that does not fit.
  • Lines split only at their units: per wave (HCX setup and pilot stay with the first), per change window, per vCenter (ELM and NSX adders with the first), per site pair and exercise, vDefend by stage, zone and application, modules and per-unit terms, depth by tier.
  • A fully displaced add-on keeps 25% of its Tier 1 value in E2 as design when it fits, and is flagged when it does not.
  • Displaced work goes to F (follow-on entitlement, depends on a future purchase), or to C (customer-funded) when the customer wants it this year.
  • Guardrails: under-funded when the core does not fit (the plan keeps the whole core and shows the gap), every hour of scope lands in exactly one bucket, required monthly burn, reserve release at month 6.
