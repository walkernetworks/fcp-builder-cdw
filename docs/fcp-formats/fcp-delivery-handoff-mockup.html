Start here: what delivery needs to know first

  1. The plan uses every remaining hour. 39 hours were delivered in assessment and discovery; the plan schedules the other 223. There is no reserve, so any change request needs a scope trade or more entitlement.
  2. CDW runs only the proving waves. Migration was cut from 126 to 41 CDW hours to fit the entitlement. Acme Health runs the remaining waves with the runbook, at a peak of 30 hours a week (0.75 FTE), Feb to Apr 2027. Confirm they can staff it.
  3. Compatibility is not established. No RVTools host rows were imported, so every host model is unverified. The current export is overdue (owner Pat Rivera) and gates design sign-off and the 1 Jan 2027 build start.
  4. 17 of 24 estimating inputs are assumed. Confirm the high-impact ones in the first design session: VM count, migration waves with dates, extended VLANs and protected VMs (page 3).
  5. Recovery objectives are undefined. The DR site is being built now, but the recovery design is follow-on work after 28 Apr 2027. Don't let DR scope creep into the entitlement.
