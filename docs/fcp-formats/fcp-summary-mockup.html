What we're doing

CDW designs and builds a new VCF 9.1 foundation for Acme Health on new hardware, proves the migration runbook on the first waves, and hands the platform and the runbook to Acme Health's team, who run the remaining waves. VCF Operations becomes the team's daily view of health and capacity. Recovery design and the first workload domain follow after the entitlement.

In this plan

  • Program management: kickoff and hardware compatibility review; assessment and discovery already delivered
  • Infrastructure: design and bill of materials, foundation build, migration plan, proving waves
  • Operations: VCF Operations design, access mapping, licensing, training, handover, runbook

Not in this plan

  • Cloud services: Kubernetes, Private AI and HCX were not requested
  • Security and resilience: recovery design and build follow in 2027
  • Remaining migration waves: run by Acme Health with the runbook

Architecture at a glance

PlatformVCF 9.1 on new Cisco UCS hosts. Converged management domain on the primary cluster, one VCF instance across the primary data center and the new DR site.
Storage, networkThe existing NetApp array over Fibre Channel stays principal storage. NSX is deployed VLAN-backed at first; overlay networking is a later phase.
RecoveryThe DR site joins as its own workload domain. The recovery design sets recovery objectives and the orchestration tool.
